01 — About

I'm a Software Engineering researcher at Delhi Technological University, pursuing M.Tech by Research under the supervision of my advisor. My work focuses on compressing Large Language Models for deployment on resource-constrained edge devices.

My research combines Knowledge Distillation and Post-Training Quantization to enable real-time fault detection in Industrial IoT environments — making AI practical without the cloud dependency.

Before this, I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science and have worked on projects spanning image processing, steganalysis, and applied machine learning.

04 — Research & Publications
Working Paper / Under Review Target: IEEE Trans. on Industrial Informatics

Efficient LLM Deployment on IIoT Edge Devices via Knowledge Distillation and Quantization

This work investigates the joint application of task-specific knowledge distillation and post-training quantization for deploying transformer-based fault detection models on compute-constrained industrial edge nodes. We demonstrate that the proposed pipeline achieves competitive accuracy with a significantly reduced inference footprint.

Preprint Code

Investigates deep learning approaches for detecting steganographic content in digital images. The modified AlexNet architecture incorporates adaptive feature extraction layers, improving sensitivity to subtle statistical artifacts introduced by common embedding algorithms.
